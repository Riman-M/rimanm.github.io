Research

Green cloud computing

Energy-aware VM consolidation that holds quality of service — the MECpVms and PbVmSp selection policies weigh energy cost and SLA violation together, not one at the other's expense.

Fog & edge computing

Deciding what belongs near the sensor and what belongs in the cloud — applied to real deployments: an elephant corridor over a railway track, a tiger alarm for endangered wildlife, low-latency identification.

Quantum simulation & ML

A current direction: bringing quantum simulation and machine learning to bear on scheduling and resource problems.

Cloud security

Securing infrastructure proactively rather than after the breach — the subject of my 2025 authored book and several filed patents.
